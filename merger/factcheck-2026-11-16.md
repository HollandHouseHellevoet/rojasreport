# Independent fact-check: fights pages, tracker, insights, headline figures

Checked November 16, 2026 against primary sources on the web (Federal Register XML, govinfo statute and bill-status files, CMS newsroom, GAO, MedPAC, OIG, HHS, PubMed/NEJM, JAMA/PMC, SEC EDGAR, PAI, KFF, HCCI, W&M committee). Where a host blocked the fetcher (hrsa.gov, cbo.gov, congress.gov, health.state.mn.us, lda.senate.gov API, NEJM/Health Affairs/JAMA sites), the row says so and names what was used instead.

**Summary: 94 items checked. 75 VERIFIED, 8 MISMATCH, 11 COULD NOT VERIFY.**

## Mismatches that matter most, first

1. **GAO quotation is not what GAO wrote (340B fight page, blockquote and body; 340B insight sources).** The page quotes GAO as saying the program "does not require that the discounts reach patients" and "does not dictate how covered entities use the revenue." GAO-26-108784 (statement p. 1, Oct. 23, 2025) says: "The 340B Program does not dictate how covered entities should use this revenue or require that these drug discounts be passed on to patients." Neither quoted string appears in the testimony. Both are paraphrases inside quotation marks.
2. **Physician-owned hospital count conflicts across the site, and the lead figure is the superseded one.** Fights/home/meta.json lead with "250+" from the FY 2027 IPPS *proposed* rule (91 FR 19668, Apr. 14, 2026, citing Blumenthal BMJ 2015). The 6001 insight cites the FY 2027 IPPS *final* rule (91 FR 50104 and n. 572, Aug. 4, 2026): "It is estimated that more than 240 hospitals are owned and operated by physicians," citing Physician-Led Healthcare for America (2026). Both quotations are exact. But the label "the estimate CMS cites" is attached to the April figure, and CMS's later, final statement is 240+. A reader who follows both links sees the site citing two CMS estimates for the same thing.
3. **H.R. 9504 reporting threshold is misstated (340B fight page, fix section; tracker note).** The page says hospitals "above $100 million in net patient revenue or 100 beds" must report 340B patients by insurance type, net 340B payment and compliance costs. In the introduced text (new IRC 6033A(c)(1)(C), (d)(2)-(3)) and the JCT description (JCX-26-26, pp. 9-10), 340B information is required only of "high revenue" organizations (net patient revenue over $100,000,000). The "large" tier (more than 100 staffed inpatient beds) carries other reporting, not 340B. The committee-reported text (ANS) could not be retrieved; the claim fails against the text that is public.
4. **Tracker row `340b-rebate` source URL does not resolve.** https://www.aha.org/lawsuit/2026-10-09-aha-lawsuit-340b-rebate-model returns 404. The filing exists at https://www.aha.org/legal-documents/2026-10-09-aha-files-340b-complaint-declaratory-and-injunctive-relief-against-hhs-hrsa (D. Me.; challenges the pilot expanded by the Aug. 3, 2026 notice, 91 Fed. Reg. 48,883, "to begin on January 1, 2027"). The build rule says every row carries a source URL; this one is dead.
5. **Tracker row `con-states` source URL does not serve a page.** https://tafhc.org/con/reform/ fails the TLS handshake (unrecognized name) and plain HTTP returns an empty body as of today. DNS resolves, nothing is published there yet.
6. **JAMA Network Open hospital count (physician-ownership page source 11; 6001 insight source 10).** The site says "1,115 other hospitals." The article says "Our final sample includes 156 POHs and 1116 non-POHs located in 78 HRRs."
7. **CMS quotation on directed referrals (physician-ownership page).** The page quotes CMS: a 90 percent directed-referral requirement "does not run afoul" of the rule. 85 FR 77550 says "it would not run afoul of the special rule at § 411.354(d)(4)." Minor, but it is inside quotation marks.
8. **CBO 340B report URL (340B fight page source 2; 340B insight source 3; Compare block).** The site links https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61730. CBO's own Oct. 23, 2025 testimony cites the report as "www.cbo.gov/publication/60661." cbo.gov blocked every fetch, so I cannot say whether 61730 also resolves; the number CBO prints is 60661.

Lesser flags (not mismatches, but worth a look): the home page's $25.47M AHA lobbying figure could not be checked against LDA (API down, 503 on every try); the powermap's Q4 2025 figure ($6.57M) is identical to the Q4 2024 figure Legis1 reports ($6,570,000). The HRSA FY 2024 audit figures (172 audits, 64 percent adverse, 24 percent repayment) could not be reached and the only secondary snapshot found (155 completed, 32 repayments) does not match them.
