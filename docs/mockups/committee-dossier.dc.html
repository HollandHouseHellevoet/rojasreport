Senate Finance

The committee that writes the rules for Medicare, Medicaid and the tax exemption every nonprofit hospital lives on. In January it changes hands, and more than a third of its Republicans are gone.

Download the committee brief (PDF) Get the member dataset
Never heard of it? Most people haven't.

What this committee does, in plain English

Congress does its real work in committees. A small group of senators writes the bills; the full Senate mostly votes yes or no on what they send it. On health care, this is the room where the bills get written.

  1. 1
    It writes Medicare's rules.
    What Medicare pays your practice, and why it pays a hospital more for the same visit. Almost every change to Medicare in the Senate starts here.
  2. 2
    It writes Medicaid's federal rules.
    How much federal money goes to each state, and the financing tricks that route much of it back to hospital systems.
  3. 3
    It writes the tax code.
    Including the exemption that lets nonprofit hospitals pay no federal income tax and borrow with tax-free bonds, while your practice pays both.
  4. 4
    Its House twin is Ways and Means.
    The same rules have to pass both. In the House, Energy and Commerce shares part of Medicare; in the Senate, the HELP Committee handles public health, drug approval and the 340B program.
