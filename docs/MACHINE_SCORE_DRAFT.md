# The Machine Score — draft formula

*Draft for Dutch's markup. November 6, 2026.*

## What it measures

One number from 0 to 100 for every member of Congress (state legislators later): **how independent the member is from the hospital lobby.** Higher is better.

- **100** = never sided with the hospital lobby. Fully independent.
- **0** = sides with the lobby every time. A vote for the machine.

It measures behavior, not party, ideology or intent. A Republican and a Democrat with the same record get the same score.

## The anchor: what counts as "the lobby's side"

Each scored action is judged against the **public position of the American Hospital Association (AHA) or the Federation of American Hospitals (FAH)** on that specific bill, amendment, rule or letter, as stated in their own letters, key-vote alerts, testimony or press releases.

Using the lobby's own published positions keeps the score defensible. We never decide what's pro-hospital; the AHA and FAH told everyone in writing. If neither group took a public position, the action isn't scored.

## What gets scored

Only actions on the **cost drivers** the site already tracks. For federal members, CON is left out because it's state law. It comes back for state legislators.

| Driver | Examples of scored actions |
|---|---|
| I. Physician-owned hospitals (Section 6001) | Bills to lift or keep the ban; letters to CMS on expansion exceptions |
| II. Price transparency | Enforcement bills; votes on penalties; letters opposing or supporting CMS rules |
| IV. Hospital subsidies | The 340B program reform; nonprofit tax exemption and charity care standards; DSH; tax-exempt bonds |
| V. CMS favoritism | Site-neutral payment bills and amendments; facility fee limits; sign-on letters to CMS |
| VI. Stark and Anti-Kickback | Bills that ease or tighten the rules that keep physicians from building competitors |

## How actions are weighted

| Action | Weight |
|---|---|
| Floor vote | 3 |
| Committee vote or markup amendment | 2 |
| Sponsoring a bill | 2 |
| Cosponsoring a bill | 1 |
| Signing a letter to CMS, HHS or leadership | 1 |
| On-the-record public statement (hearing, release) | 1 |

**Recency:** actions from the last three Congresses (six years) count in full. Older actions count half.

## The formula

> **Score = 100 × (weighted actions against the lobby's position) ÷ (all weighted scored actions)**

**Minimum record:** at least **5 scored actions**. Below that the member shows "Insufficient record," not a number. New senators like Adam Hamilton and Roy Cooper start here.

**Worked example (hypothetical):** a senator has 2 floor votes with the lobby (6), 1 committee vote against it (2), and cosponsored 2 lobby-backed bills (2).
Against the lobby = 2. Total = 6 + 2 + 2 = 10. **Score = 20.**

**First real test, Rep. Kevin Hern (R-Okla.):** 3 verified actions, all against the lobby (floor vote for H.R. 5378, sponsored the FAIR Act, co-led the physician-owned hospital bill). **Score = 100**, provisional until he has 5 scored actions.

## Bands (names are editorial: rename freely)

| Score | Band |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | Reformer |
| 60–79 | Independent |
| 40–59 | Mixed |
| 20–39 | Reliable ally of the lobby |
| 0–19 | The Machine |

## Money: my recommendation is to show it beside the score, not inside it

Hospital-industry PAC and executive contributions (FEC data) belong on every member page. I recommend **not** folding them into the score:

- **Why:** the score's strength is that it rests only on what members *did*, judged against what the lobby *asked for in writing*. Mix in money and every member with a low score says the number just means "took hospital money," and the record gets lost in that argument.
- **Instead:** show "Hospital money, career: $X (rank among 535 members)" right next to the score. Readers make the connection themselves, and it's stronger that way.
- **Alternative if you want it in:** cap money at 15% of the score, as a percentile rank of hospital-industry receipts among all members.

## Publication rules

1. Every scored action is listed on the member's page with a link to the vote, bill or letter **and** to the AHA or FAH document stating the lobby's position.
2. Scores are recalculated whenever a scored action happens; the page shows "as of" dates.
3. Any member's office can submit a correction or statement. Corrections are logged publicly.
4. The full list of scored actions is published as a download, so anyone can reproduce any score.
