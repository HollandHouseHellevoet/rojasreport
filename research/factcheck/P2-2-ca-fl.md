## Method notes

- Money and tax figures: recomputed over the 182 California and 55 Florida rows flagged `inCostReport` in the pipeline files. Federal tax = round(0.21 x sum of positive net income); state tax = round(rate x same base), CA 0.0884 and FL 0.055; property tax = land, land improvements and buildings at cost x rate per hospital, CA Los Angeles 0.01187 for county LOS ANGELES or a blank county with city LOS ANGELES or WEST HILLS (38 hospitals), Yreka 0.01061 elsewhere; FL landBuildingRate 0.020165 for county DUVAL (3 hospitals), 0.024415 elsewhere. Rates match `tax_rates.json`.
- Public hospital counts: CMS type of control codes 8 to 13 (governmental) on the cost-report rows.
- Dual classification: rows with `ruralUrban` = R and `section401` = true; the 34 and 18 names match the page's lists one for one.
- Copy rules: every reader-facing string (verdict, text, note, body, status, title, subtitle, heading, role, name, asOf, label) split into sentences with an abbreviation-aware splitter and word-counted; searched for em dashes, "should", "doctor", "provider", bare "340B", and the owner-company and client names.
