      Republican James Uthmeier wins reelection for attorney general in Florida

      Prev Next
      WPTV
      Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier speaks in West Palm Beach on Nov. 3, 2026, after winning reelection.
      By: Michael Hoffman

      WEST PALM BEACH, Fla. — Republican James Uthmeier won Florida's attorney general race Tuesday night, securing another term after a closely contested election.

      Uthmeier, who entered the race as the sitting attorney general, won by about 2 percentage points, according to the latest results.

      Speaking to supporters after claiming victory, Uthmeier pointed to his record in office, highlighting arrests and convictions under his leadership and arguing that his experience showed voters he could continue doing the job.

      He later spoke with WPTV about what the narrow margins in his race and other Republican contests could mean for the future of the GOP in Florida.

      "I think Republicans are learning that people across the country, they're facing challenges. They're struggling," Uthmeier said.

      WATCH BELOW: Uthmeier speaks about winning attorney general's race

      James Uthmeier claims victory in Florida attorney general's race

      Uthmeier acknowledged the financial pressures families are facing, saying he experiences some of those same concerns.

      "Look, I've got four little ones at home and I face challenges at the gas pump too. I get it," he said. "But at the end of the day, what we learned tonight in our race is that we ultimately have to have public safety."

      Public safety was a central theme of Uthmeier's campaign and his election-night remarks.

      Despite the narrow margin, the reporter said Uthmeier received approximately 4.1 million votes, potentially the highest vote total among Florida Cabinet candidates on the ballot this election.
